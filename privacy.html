Next Galaxy - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Next Galaxy ("the game") is published by Spica Studio ("we"). Contact: spicatech1@gmail.com.

What we collect

The game has no account, no sign-in and no chat. Your progress (missions, chips, jets, supplies, lives, settings) is stored only on your device.

The game uses Google services that may collect data from your device as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA/UK a consent form (Google UMP) asks for your choice first. You can reset or disable ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads. See Google's advertising policy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional jet purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google Play tells the game which products you own so they can be unlocked and restored.

Diagnostics

Google Play services and the ad SDK may collect crash logs and diagnostic information to keep the service stable.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold your game data on our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes the local save. For data held by Google, use your Google account controls.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
